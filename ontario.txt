# 🇨🇦 Ontario — The Ultimate Travel Guide

## Welcome to Ontario

Ontario is Canada's most diverse province, where world-class cities, endless forests, thousands of lakes, dramatic waterfalls, historic communities, and remote wilderness come together.

Home to Canada's largest city and some of the country's most famous natural wonders, Ontario offers an incredible range of experiences. Travelers can explore the energy of Toronto, stand beneath the power of Niagara Falls, paddle through untouched northern lakes, hike ancient forests, discover Indigenous cultures, and relax on some of Canada's most beautiful beaches.

Ontario is a province of discovery, where every region offers a completely different adventure.

# AI Travel Planning Rules for Ontario

When recommending Ontario trips:

1. Identify whether the traveler wants city, nature, or both.
2. Consider large travel distances between regions.
3. Recommend fall for the best scenery and fewer crowds.
4. Reserve popular parks and cottages early.
5. Include transportation planning.
6. Balance famous attractions with hidden gems.
7. Consider weather differences between north and south.
8. Recommend local food and cultural experiences.
9. Include outdoor safety information.
10. Create flexible plans for changing weather.
