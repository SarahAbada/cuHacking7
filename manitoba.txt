# 🇨🇦 Manitoba — The Ultimate Travel Guide

## Welcome to Manitoba

Manitoba is Canada's gateway between the eastern and western provinces, a land of endless prairies, ancient boreal forests, thousands of lakes, powerful rivers, and some of the most incredible wildlife experiences on Earth.

Known as the **Heart of Canada**, Manitoba offers a rare combination of urban culture, Indigenous heritage, untouched wilderness, and unforgettable natural encounters.

From watching polar bears walk along the shores of Hudson Bay to kayaking through remote northern waters, Manitoba gives travelers access to experiences found nowhere else.

It is a province of big skies, wild landscapes, and extraordinary adventures.
